About Me

Hello! I am Tanim Ahmmod, a recent Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), Bangladesh.

My thesis work at the SPML Lab focused on machine learning and deep learning-based biomedical signal processing. I worked extensively with Shimmer devices to capture real-time PPG, ECG, and GSR data for dataset generation. To make AI deployable on edge devices, I developed lightweight deep learning models via Knowledge Distillation for Bundle Branch Block (BBB) detection and implemented the Aftershock Decay Optimization (ADO) algorithm for hyperparameter tuning.

Currently, I am actively seeking MSc / PhD opportunities with Graduate Assistantships to advance the integration of AI in biomedical applications and complex engineering systems.
