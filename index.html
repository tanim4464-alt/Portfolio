Electrical & Electronic Engineering Student

I'm Tanim Ahmmod, a recent graduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My prior research and development projects involved advanced iontophoresis and non-invasive blood glucose monitoring devices. During my thesis work at the (Signal Processing Machine Learning) SPML Lab, I focused on machine learning and deep learning-based biomedical signal processing. My work included utilizing Shimmer devices to collect real-time PPG, ECG, and GSR data for dataset generation, alongside developing lightweight deep learning models via knowledge distillation for Bundle Branch Block detection. Additionally, I implemented the Aftershock Decay Optimization (ADO) algorithm for hyperparameter tuning.

Beyond biomedical applications, I am deeply passionate about machine learning, deep learning, and their integration across diverse domains such as ground blasting, tumor type classification, and uniaxial compressive strength prediction. My technical expertise spans PyTorch, TensorFlow, MATLAB, LaTeX, and advanced signal processing tools.

I am an IEEE Student Member and have presented my research at multiple international IEEE conferences, including SPICSCON 2026, ICCIT 2025, and CECTE 2026. Currently, I am eager to pursue an MSc or PhD with a Graduate Assistantship, aiming to advance the integration of AI in biomedical applications and engineering systems through cutting-edge research and impactful education.
