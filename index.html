I am Tanim Ahmmod, an Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) student at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. As a Research Assistant at the Signal Processing & Machine Learning (SPML) Lab, my focus spans biomedical signal processing, machine learning, and deep learning architectures.

Key Focus Areas & Research Highlights:

  • Biomedical Applications: Developed an iontophoresis device, non-invasive glucose monitoring system, tumor classification models, and collected PPG, ECG, and GSR data via Shimmer devices.
  • Machine Learning Models: Knowledge distillation, lightweight model development, novel optimization algorithms, fuzzy analysis, and control branch feature extractor.
  • Cross-Domain ML: Ground blasting and uniaxial compressive strength prediction.
  • Technical Stack: PyTorch, TensorFlow, MATLAB, LaTeX, and signal processing tools.

An IEEE Student Member with presentations at SPICSCON 2026, ICCIT 2025, and CECTE 2026, I am actively seeking MSc/PhD opportunities with a Graduate Assistantship to advance AI integration in biomedical and engineering systems.
